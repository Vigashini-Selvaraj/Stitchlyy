Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

  • 1. Information Collection

1. Information We Collect

When you use the STITCHLY platform, we collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, shipping address, and your precise body measurements required for bespoke tailoring.

We also automatically collect standard web analytics data, such as your IP address, browser type, and interactions with our website to improve our user experience. Financial data is processed securely via our trusted payment gateways and is never stored directly on our servers.

2. Data Usage & Processing

The primary use of your data, specifically your body measurements, is to fulfill your bespoke clothing orders. Your measurements are securely stored in your personal profile for future purchases to ensure a seamless experience.

We may also use your email to send you updates regarding your order status, new collection launches, and exclusive promotional offers. You may opt-out of marketing communications at any time. We do not engage in the sale or unauthorized sharing of your personal profiling data.

3. Your Data Rights

STITCHLY employs industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your personal and payment information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

You reserve the right to access, modify, or completely delete your personal data (including your saved measurements) from our servers at any time by accessing your account dashboard or contacting our support team. Data removal requests are processed within 7 business days.
