Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our tailoring process, orders, and services.

1. Custom Tailoring Process

We provide a comprehensive Measurement Guide complete with step-by-step video tutorials. You can follow this guide to measure yourself, or you can book a free virtual consultation where one of our master tailors will guide you through the process over video call to ensure absolute precision.

2. Orders & Shipping

Because every garment is bespoke and crafted entirely from scratch, our standard construction turnaround time is 2-3 weeks from the moment your measurements and fabric choices are finalized. Intricate embroidery may add an additional week.

Absolutely! STITCHLY ships worldwide. International shipping usually takes an additional 5-10 business days depending on customs processing in your destination country. All packages are fully insured.

3. Fabrics & Care

Yes, we offer a dedicated 'Stitch Only' service. You can mail us your own premium fabric, and our artisans will craft it into your desired design based on your measurements. Contact support to initiate a Stitch Only order.

We recommend professional dry cleaning for all our bespoke silk, wool, and embellished garments to maintain their structure and longevity. For pure cottons and linens, a gentle cold hand-wash is acceptable. Detailed, fabric-specific care instructions are included in the packaging of every delivery.
