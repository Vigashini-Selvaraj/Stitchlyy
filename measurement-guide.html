Perfect Fit Starts With the Right Measurements

Follow our simple measurement guide to get accurate measurements for your custom STITCHLY creation.

Start Measuring Custom Stitching

For the Most Accurate Measurements

  • Wear lightweight, fitted clothing
  • Stand naturally and keep your posture relaxed
  • Use a soft measuring tape
  • Keep the tape snug, but don't pull tightly
  • Ask someone to help for better accuracy
  • Measure twice before submitting

Essential Measurements for Women

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust while keeping the tape level across your back.

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist, typically just above the belly button.

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips and buttocks, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.

Shoulder

Measure across the back from the edge of one shoulder bone to the edge of the other.

Armhole

Wrap the tape around the shoulder joint, under the armpit and over the top of the shoulder.

Sleeve Length

Measure from the top edge of the shoulder bone down to your desired sleeve length (elbow, wrist).

Garment Length

Measure straight down from the highest point of your shoulder to the desired length of the outfit.

Neck

Measure around the base of your neck where a collar would naturally sit, leaving room for a finger.

Essential Measurements for Men

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your armpits, keeping the tape level.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, where you typically wear your trousers or pants.

Shoulder

Measure across the upper back from the end of one shoulder bone to the other.

Neck

Measure around the base of the neck, keeping one finger between the tape and the neck for comfort.

Sleeve Length

Measure from the top of the shoulder seam down to the wrist bone.

Shirt / Kurta Length

Measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the desired hem length.

Inseam

Measure from the lowest part of the crotch down to the bottom hem of the trouser leg.

Essential Measurements for Kids

Chest

Measure gently around the fullest part of the child's chest, ensuring they are breathing normally.

Waist

Measure around the natural waistline, usually just above the belly button.

Shoulder

Measure across the back from shoulder edge to shoulder edge.

Total Length

Measure from the top of the shoulder down to the knee or ankle, depending on the outfit.
